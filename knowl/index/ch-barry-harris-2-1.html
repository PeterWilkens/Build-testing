This chapter presents the harmonic and theoretical system developed by the Detroit jazz pianist and educator Barry Harris (1929–2021). Harris spent more than six decades teaching jazz in workshops around the world, and his approach offers one of the most coherent and practical frameworks for understanding bebop harmony ever assembled. His system is centred on two complementary ideas: the bebop scale and the 6th chord / diminished 7th system.
