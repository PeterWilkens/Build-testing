Before discussing scales it is useful to understand how the distance between notes is described. The musical distance between notes on your instrument keyboard or on a musical staff is known as an interval. This interval is dependent on the number of semitones between notes and is described as a minor 2nd, a major 2nd, a minor 3rd, a major 3rd, a 5th, a 7th, etc.
