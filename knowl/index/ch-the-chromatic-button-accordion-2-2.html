In the most common type of chromatic accordion, with a Stradella bass, the bass and chord buttons are arranged in six columns parallel to the bellows. The columns are: Counter Bass, Bass, Major Chord, Minor Chord, 7th Chord and Diminished Chord. The basses are arranged in intervals of a fifth and the chords are built on the second row of basses. See Section 1.2 for detailed arrangement of the keys on Stradella Bass instrument.
