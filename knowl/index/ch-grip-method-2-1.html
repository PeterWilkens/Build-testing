The Grip Method gives you four moveable chord shapes — Grips 1a, 2a, 1b, and 2b — that cover every rootless major-7th and Lydian-dominant (maj7♭5) voicing across the whole keyboard. Category A grips (1a and 2a) stack the chord tones 3–5–7–9 ascending from the bottom button. Category B grips (1b and 2b) re-voice the same four tones as 7–9–3–5, keeping the lower two notes in place while the upper two move up by an octave. Moving any grip up or down the isometric layout transposes it without changing its fingering shape.
